# Secondary comparison: can a capped forecast update be certified on continuous states?

Specified before the pendulum test results. Reuse the two held-out full-observation map-A schedules, all five independent fit seeds, and the training/validation choices of the main study. Compare (a) the validation-selected classical model as an update to persistence, and (b) the validation-selected learned model as an update to the classical selection. These are distinct baselines, not independent trials. Cap the absolute forecast change at 0.10, 0.30, or the full observable range 2.00. Evaluate the **same** capped forecasts with the generic and cap-specific range bounds, on the same 4,096 calibration starts and horizons 1–6. Split a separate 0.05 failure budget over the two schedule × two baseline/proposal pairs × two bound methods per fitted seed, then cover the three caps and six horizons within each comparison. This is an auxiliary statistical family and is not used for the primary training gate. Report certified positive gains by cap, sixth-horizon test risk, and any inconclusive cells. Neither the finite-support shortcut nor a distribution-free conclusion about a single dependent rollout is allowed.

Also compare two prespecified methods for the *absolute* threshold on clipped persistence, validation-selected classical, and validation-selected learned forecasts at RMSE 0.15. Use the same paired continuations and starts. Split a separate auxiliary family 0.05 over two primary schedules and two threshold procedures (then the three models and six horizons), per fitted seed. One procedure uses the empirical Bernstein upper bound; the other tests the threshold directly using a fixed equal mixture of five bets. Record contiguous accepted horizons, not just total isolated acceptances. This does not replace the primary all-candidate empirical Bernstein gate after observing results.
