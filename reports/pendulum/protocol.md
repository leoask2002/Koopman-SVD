# Fixed protocol: stochastic pendulum transfer

Written before computing candidate test errors. This is a small model-development study, not a registered clinical/statistical trial. Its aim is an accuracy/compression decision, not a claim about identifying the continuous generator.

- **System:** six 0.25-unit forecast intervals of a dimensionless noisy damped pendulum; origin zero. Target is cosine of angle in [−1,1]. Damping schedule is available exogenously to every method at prediction time. No method fits test transitions. See the [simulator specification](../../experiments/pendulum_system.py).
- **Domains:** four training schedules (low, high, ramp, step), two other validation schedules (smooth, switch), and two *different* smooth/switch schedules for test and calibration. Training-map A is an 8-channel sensor with nonlinear channels and informative physical anchors. Apply its trained models unchanged to nuisance-map B. Separately fit partial-observation map A without velocity. The B test retains anchors and is not a completely uncalibrated sensor transfer. Use the same latent start paths and noise across corresponding views where possible.
- **Units and budget:** use seeds 71–75, with 256 independent training trajectories per training schedule and 256 independent validation trajectories per validation schedule. Fit once per view and seed. For each held-out schedule, 512 independent test starts and 128 Monte Carlo continuations per start estimate the conditional mean. Subtract the finite-replica reference variance from the mean squared error before taking the root; retain signed MSE, within-start noise floor and standard error over independent test starts. For angle-only starts independently resample the hidden initial velocity and forcing on *each* branch. Held-out target continuations supply neither feature fitting nor model selection.
- **Fixed candidates:** persistence; pooled, local/smoothed and low-rank EDMD using sensor and domain-aware dictionaries; direct horizon regression; frozen vs nested-loss neural dictionaries at total ranks 4 and 8 (64 CPU updates per fit, one hidden layer of width 16); matched raw sensor spans; a bounded official-objective comparison where available. Constants count toward rank. Explicitly distinguish shared/two-sided feature learning from fitted forecast heads and from a full upstream training reproduction. Validation sees held-out noisy endpoints. Choose each family winner by mean clipped forecast squared error at horizon six across both validation schedules; ties broken deterministically by name. Apply unchanged to all test scenarios for the corresponding observed training view.
- **Primary endpoint:** full A, horizon six. For each seed and model, average the two independent schedule-specific corrected squared risks, then take one square root. Report mean across seeds; use paired seed bootstraps to summarize the difference and relative RMSE, with five independent fits and common test starts/continuations among methods. Treat intervals as descriptive and limited to these two schedules; display both schedules and all stress arms separately.
- **Certification:** 4,096 new independent calibration starts per schedule/view, two independent conditional branches per start. Use clipped outputs and the empirical Bernstein bounds without assuming finite support. Allocate failure probability 0.05/12 to each absolute-risk and improvement family in each of six cases, simultaneously over all fixed models and horizons within a case (per fitted seed, not simultaneously over seeds). Report accepted contiguous prefix from time zero at absolute RMSE tolerance 0.15 and lower bounds on risk improvement relative to the classical model selected on validation. Endpoints at different horizons are not independent replicates.
- **Scale-up gate:** primary neural model must either reduce primary RMSE by more than 10% versus validation-selected strongest classical, or fall within 5% relative RMSE with at least 2× fewer propagated coordinates and lower measured forecasting time. Also require all six horizons to certify at 0.15 on both primary schedules for every fit. Propagated rank, feature width, trainable parameter count, inference latency and model storage are different; where a baseline predicts directly it does not propagate a latent state, and no rank compression win may be asserted. Median single-thread CPU time for the same 128 starts and six forecasts after warm-up is descriptive, not portable benchmarking. A favorable point estimate is exploratory; uncertainty, failure counts and negative results must accompany it.
- **Decision:** if the gate fails, do not scale neural training on these results. Choose a simpler working forecast/dictionary or abstain at the desired horizon. The primary is fixed; post hoc observations require a fresh system/seed panel.
